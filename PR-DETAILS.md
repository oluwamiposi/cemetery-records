## Overview

Blockchain-based cemetery record management system for digitizing burial records, plot ownership, and genealogical information.

## Contract Details

### burial-recorder (250+ lines)

**Features:**
- Burial record management with memorial inscriptions
- Plot ownership and transfer tracking
- Family connection mapping for genealogy
- Surname-based genealogy indexing
- Plot availability tracking

**Key Functions:**
- `create-plot`: Create cemetery plots
- `purchase-plot`: Purchase plot ownership
- `record-burial`: Record burials with full details
- `add-family-connection`: Map family relationships
- `update-genealogy-index`: Index by surname
- `transfer-plot-ownership`: Transfer plot ownership
- `update-memorial-inscription`: Update memorials
